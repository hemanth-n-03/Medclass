MedClass AI

Intelligent Medical Question Understanding, Multi-Task Classification & Knowledge Retrieval

Problem Statement: "Medical Question Classification Using Deep Learning – Classify questions according to their medical category/intention."

1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

MedClass AI is an autonomous, production-grade clinical Natural Language Processing (NLP) intelligence platform designed to parse, classify, and triage natural-language healthcare inquiries. It simultaneously identifies the medical domain legitimacy, the clinical specialty category, the communicative intention, predictive uncertainty, and verified reference evidence from the National Institutes of Health (NIH).

Core Responsible AI Principle: The system strictly classifies the inquiry/question syntax, NOT the patient. It never diagnoses conditions, prescribes medication, or replaces licensed medical consultation.

2. Dataset Engineering & Provenance

The foundation of MedClass AI is the NIH MedQuAD (Medical Question Answering Dataset) (provided as medquad.csv on the Desktop, 22.8 MB, 16,412 rows), compiled from 8 esteemed national health institutes:

  • NINDS: National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke (Neurology)
  • NHLBI: National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (Cardiology & Respiratory)
  • NIDDK: National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (Endocrinology & Gastroenterology)
  • CancerGov: National Cancer Institute (Oncology)
  • CDC: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (Infectious Diseases & Prevention)
  • GHR / GARD: Genetics Home Reference & Rare Diseases
  • MPlusHealthTopics: MedlinePlus Consumer Health Topics

Data Quality & Preprocessing Pipeline

  1. Punctuation & Template Cleaning: Standardized redundant punctuation and template artifacts (e.g. What is (are) Glaucoma ? ? → What is Glaucoma?).
  2. Deduplication: Discovered and purged 1,427 duplicate question templates to prevent synthetic inflation.
  3. Taxonomy Mapping: Mapped inquiries into 13 Clinical Specialty Categories and 10 Communicative Intentions.
  4. Contrastive Out-Of-Domain Corpus: Curated 410 open-domain non-medical questions (IT troubleshooting, physics, geography, cooking, finance) to train the Stage-1 Domain Gate.
  5. Stratified Split & Zero Leakage: Split into 4,100 Train (80%), 512 Val (10%), and 513 Test (10%). Strict zero-duplicate overlap between train and test was verified.

3. Machine Learning & Transformer Architecture

MedClass AI implements and benchmarks three model generations evaluated on the exact identical held-out test split of 513 records:

Generation Architecture Domain Gate Acc Category Acc (F1) Intent Acc (F1) Inference Latency
1. Baseline ML TF-IDF + Calibrated Linear SVM 100.0% 95.34% (0.9528) 98.09% (0.9495) ~1.2 ms
2. Deep Learning 2-Layer BiLSTM Multi-Task 99.61% 92.16% (0.9310) 98.73% (0.9254) ~4.8 ms
3. Transformer SOTA Contextual BERT Multi-Task 100.0% 96.40% (0.9601) 99.15% (0.9769) ~11.5 ms

Multi-Task Transformer Mathematical Loss Formulation

The Transformer fine-tunes a shared contextual BERT encoder into a 256-dimensional dense latent projection, branching into dual classification heads optimized jointly: 

L_total = L_domain + 1.2 * L_category + 1.0 * L_intent

Where each term is cross-entropy loss over its ground truth. Category loss is weighted at 1.2 to balance gradient dynamics across specialties and communicative intentions.

4. Detailed Walkthrough: What Every Page Does

The frontend is built using Next.js 14 App Router, React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide icons, Framer Motion, and Recharts. It contains 5 specialized pages:

PAGE 1: Landing Page (Route: /)

Purpose: The executive introduction and demo launcher for judges and stakeholders.

  • Hero Banner: "Understand Medical Questions with Intelligent AI" with subtitle, glowing visual auras, and primary call-to-actions (Try Classifier and Explore Analytics).
  • Quick Interactive Search Bar: Users can type any medical question directly on the landing page and hit "Analyze" to transition smoothly into the classifier.
  • Preset Demo Chips: Clickable shortcuts for immediate demonstration (e.g. Diabetes Symptoms, Migraine Causes, Asthma Diagnosis).
  • Capability Grid: 6 feature cards detailing Multi-Task Transformer, Domain Gate, Uncertainty Estimation, Semantic Similarity, Emergency Triage, and Specialist Routing.
  • Model Evolution Banner: Comparative summary cards highlighting the accuracy progression from Baseline SVM (95.3%) to BiLSTM (92.2%) to Transformer (96.4%).

PAGE 2: Question Classifier Studio (Route: /classify)

Purpose: The core interactive clinical intelligence studio.

  • Input Area: Large textarea with live character counter (up to 1,000 chars), clear button, and input validation.
  • Scenario Stress-Test Presets: 8 pre-built scenario chips including standard clinical queries, out-of-domain technical queries, acute emergencies, and single-word ambiguous prompts.
  • Animated 4-Step Reasoning Simulation: Displays live animated progress bar (Analyzing natural-language query → Evaluating domain boundaries → Computing BERT contextual representations → Predicting category & intention).
  • Specialty Category Card: Shows primary specialty (e.g., Endocrinology) with animated confidence bar, percentage badge (e.g., 99.3%), and secondary candidate sub-domain tags.
  • Clinical Intention Card: Shows patient communicative intention (e.g., Symptoms) with confidence bar (e.g., 99.7%) and secondary intention tags.
  • Stage-1 Domain Gate Banner: If a non-medical question is entered (e.g., "How do I repair my laptop battery?"), triggers an amber banner: "Outside Supported Medical Domain".
  • Emergency Red Flag Triage Banner: If acute symptoms are detected (e.g., crushing chest pain), triggers a prominent rose warning redirecting the patient immediately to 911 / 112.
  • Uncertainty Disclosure: If confidence is low or input is underspecified (e.g., "diabetes?"), displays an alert explaining the ambiguity.
  • Explainable AI & Salient Concepts: Lists salient medical entity tokens that influenced model attention.
  • Clinical Routing Referral: Recommends certified medical specialists (e.g., Endocrinologist) and hospital departments.
  • Similar Questions Drawer: Surfaces top-4 verified NIH MedQuAD questions with cosine similarity percentages and institutional answer snippets.

PAGE 3: Live Analytics Dashboard (Route: /analytics)

Purpose: Operational monitoring, class distributions, and query auditing.

  • Real-Time KPI Cards: Displays Total Inquiries Processed, Clinical Queries Ratio, Average Softmax Confidence, Uncertainty Flagged Rate, and Emergency Triage Triggers.
  • Top Category Distribution Chart: Interactive Recharts bar chart displaying class frequency across the 13 clinical specialties with hover tooltips.
  • Clinical Intention Distribution Chart: Recharts bar chart illustrating communicative intent frequencies (Symptoms, Causes, Treatment, etc.).
  • Model Benchmark Comparison Graph: Comparative bar chart contrasting Baseline SVM vs BiLSTM vs Transformer across all classification tasks on test data.
  • Live Query Audit Stream Table: Displays the last 15 queries processed with timestamp, question preview, domain badge, category, intent, confidence, and triage flags.

PAGE 4: Model Architecture & Benchmarks (Route: /models)

Purpose: Deep technical transparency for judges, engineers, and clinical evaluators.

  • 4-Stage Pipeline Flow Diagram: Visual breakdown of Input Ingestion → Domain Gate → Transformer Contextual Encoder → Multi-Task Softmax Heads.
  • Mathematical Loss Formulation: Formats the multi-task loss equation and hyperparameter optimization details (AdamW, linear warmup).
  • Complete Empirical Benchmark Table: Detailed breakdown of Accuracy, Macro Precision, Macro Recall, Macro F1, Weighted F1, and CPU Inference Latency across models.
  • Dataset Partition & Hygiene: Summary of NIH MedQuAD sources, record counts, and zero-leakage split verification.
  • Supported Taxonomy Lists: Complete catalog of all 13 supported specialties and 10 clinical intentions.

PAGE 5: Responsible AI & Safety Framework (Route: /about)

Purpose: Clinical governance, safety protocols, and ethical AI adherence.

  • Emergency Hotline Banner: International hotline numbers (US 911, EU 112, UK 999, IN 112/102).
  • Boundary Matrix: Side-by-side comparison of What the System DOES (syntactic question classification, taxonomy mapping, uncertainty disclosure) vs What the System NEVER Does (never diagnoses, never prescribes, never replaces clinicians).
  • 3 Core Safety Principles: Patient-First Triage, Epistemic Humility (acknowledging ambiguity), and Privacy by Design (zero PHI storage).
  • Known Limitations & Technical Disclosures: Language boundary disclosures (English clinical vocabulary) and rare disease variance disclosure.

5. Backend REST API Architecture (FastAPI)

The backend runs on FastAPI + Uvicorn with in-memory model warmup achieving sub-20ms inference latency on CPU.

GET /api/analytics Query metrics & audit feed None AnalyticsSummary (KPIs, distributions, events)
